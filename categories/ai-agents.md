## Highlighted starting points

- [Agent Zero](https://github.com/agent0ai/agent-zero) - Autonomous general-purpose AI framework supporting tool creation, computer use and multi-agent task execution.
- [Buzz](https://github.com/block/buzz) - Open-source team workspace for humans and AI agents to collaborate through conversations and workflows.
- [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent) - General-purpose AI agent by Nous Research with persistent memory, learned skills and tool use.
- [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) - Open-source personal AI assistant that connects messaging, applications and tools to automate tasks.
- [OpenHands](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands) - AI-driven software development platform and control center for coding agents and automations.
- [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip) - Self-hosted orchestration platform for coordinating multiple AI agents, goals, tasks, budgets and approvals.
